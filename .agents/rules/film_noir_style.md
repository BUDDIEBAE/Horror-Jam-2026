# Art Direction & Visual Style: Film Noir (Pure Monochrome)

1. **Color Palette Constraint:**
   - **Monochrome Only (Black & White):** The game is strictly **Film Noir**. Do NOT use colors (no red, cyan, amber, green, blue, etc.).
   - Visual tones are limited to:
     - Pure Pitch Black (`#000000`)
     - Stark High-Contrast White (`#FFFFFF` or `#F5F8FA`)
     - Ash-Silver & Grayscale midtones (`#808080`, `#C0C0C0`, `#303030`) for hierarchy, scanlines, and faint telemetry.

2. **UI & HUD Elements:**
   - All HUD graphics, icons, text, meters, warning alerts, and trackers must adhere to monochrome styling.
   - Danger, warnings, and signals must be conveyed through **flashing frequency, contrast inversion, scanline static, pulse speed, or typography**, NEVER by switching to red/yellow color.

3. **Visual Aesthetics:**
   - Chiaroscuro lighting (harsh single-source light vs deep pitch darkness).
   - PSX retro dithering, monochrome scanlines, static noise, and CRT/radar phosphor aesthetics in white/grayscale.
